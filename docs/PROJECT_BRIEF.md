# Project brief — Coral Studio

## Goal

Create **iajaykumar.live** as the home base for Ajay Kumar's next chapter as a public speaker, technical YouTuber, and course creator. His decade of full-stack engineering and Flutter/open-source work is the proof behind the teaching. Firebase project `iajaykumarlive` is the intended host when implementation begins.

## Positioning

**Ajay Kumar — engineer, builder, and teacher.** His [current GitHub profile](https://github.com/ProjectAJ14) describes end-to-end product work with React/TypeScript, Java/Spring Boot, cloud, and mobile, plus writing and technical sessions. The site should feel like a creator's studio: a place to watch, learn, invite Ajay to speak, and explore the products behind his ideas.

Proposed hero copy for Ajay to edit: **“I build software, then share what the journey taught me.”** Supporting line: **“Full-stack engineer sharing practical lessons through talks, videos, writing, and hands-on learning.”** These are draft lines, not quotations or claims about a course or podcast already launched.

## Chosen inspiration

[Josh W. Comeau's site](https://www.joshwcomeau.com/) remains the experience reference for warmth, clarity, and interactive teaching. Ajay's site has its own studio setting, coral shirt palette, illustrated portrait, microphone/camera/lesson props, and speaking-first information hierarchy. Do not reuse Josh's artwork, copy, code, branding, or Wotfard font.

## Audience

1. **Event organizers** need speaking topics, prior sessions, and an easy invitation path.
2. **Viewers and learners** need videos, workshops, articles, and eventually courses.
3. **Developers and collaborators** need projects and open-source credibility.

The first screen should answer who Ajay is, what visitors can learn from him, and how to watch or invite him.

## Content maturity

- **Published now:** linked Medium articles, GitHub projects, YouTube channel, and talk/workshop titles from GitHub.
- **Needs curation:** direct video URLs, talk recordings/slides, event dates, course details.
- **Future possibility:** podcast/audio series. Give the visual system room for it, but do not publish a podcast page or imply episodes exist until Ajay provides them.

## First release scope

Home, Watch, Speaking, Learn, Writing, Projects, About, and Contact. Watch can begin as a curated link to the verified YouTube channel and any confirmed videos. Learn combines workshops and articles until a real course is ready. Speaking presents topics and booking information without inventing talks or testimonials.

## Explicitly deferred

Application code, Firebase configuration, DNS, deployment, newsletter collection, media hosting, podcast launch, course commerce, and a custom video player. This repository is a no-code design and content handoff.
